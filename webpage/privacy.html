Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Information accessed

The application requests read-only Gmail access to retrieve university email notifications relevant to its owner.

Use of information

Email information is processed to identify relevant notifications and deliver them to the owner's WhatsApp account.

Sharing and storage

Relevant notification content may be sent to WhatsApp through the WhatsApp Business API. The application may store message identifiers to prevent duplicate notifications. Access tokens and application credentials are used to operate the service.

Security and retention

The application is intended for personal use. Credentials should be stored securely and access should be restricted to the owner. Stored data is retained only as needed for the application's operation and is deleted when no longer needed.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application developer using the support email shown on the application's Google consent screen.
